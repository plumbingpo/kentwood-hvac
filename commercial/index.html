Food Service Comfort

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units have to replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Kentwood HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safety

Professional Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Every hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it means overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Kentwood HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Kentwood and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on rooftop and split systems. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. Once it's running, we set up maintenance and monitoring so it doesn't happen again, and each response is fully documented for your records.
